Herbot Bot is a programming puzzle game about one idea: say more with less.

Guide a little robot across a 25 × 25 board and light every target. You describe the route in H, a tiny language with three commands: s steps forward, l turns left and r turns right. Every letter and number costs one byte, so each lesson asks for a program that is not only correct, but short.

LEARN BY PLAYING
• 30 original lessons in six chapters, from a single step to recursive systems
• Discover procedures, parameters, commands passed as arguments and recursion
• Every lesson has a clear goal and two optional hints, never a full answer
• A built-in guide explains the whole language with examples

WATCH YOUR IDEAS RUN
• Run, pause or step through your program and follow the trail it leaves
• Choose 1×, 4×, 16× or Turbo speed
• Walls block the robot; traps clear every target you have lit
• Modern and Classic board styles, optional grid dots and movement trails
• Pinch to zoom and drag the board on iPhone and iPad; press ⌘Return to run on Mac

EVERY BYTE COUNTS
• Your shortest solution for each lesson is saved automatically
• Drafts are kept as you type and restored when you return
• Export and import JSON backups to move progress between devices

AI BATTLEFIELD (OPTIONAL)
Curious how AI models handle the same puzzles? Add a provider you already use (OpenRouter, SiliconFlow, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible or Anthropic-compatible API) with your own API key, choose models and puzzles, and start a match. Every submitted program is judged on your device, with retries, timing, token usage and a shareable result card. The app contacts a provider only when you save it or agree to start a match, sending requests directly with your key, which stays in the Keychain. AI Battlefield requires your own provider account, and the provider may charge you for its use; the rest of the game works without it.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Lessons, progress and settings stay on your device. Available in English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.

Herbot Bot independently implements the public rules of the Herbert programming challenge, created by Wild Noodle Corporation, whose founder Brian Conte developed the H language. It is not affiliated with Wild Noodle or Microsoft. All lessons in this app are original designs, and its source code is open on GitHub.
