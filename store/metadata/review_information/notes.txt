HERBOT BOT 2.0.0 (15) - APP REVIEW NOTES

BINARY IDENTITY
The product is Herbot Bot.app; its display, bundle and executable names are Herbot Bot. Bundle ID remains info.hugogu.Herbert.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 1.1.0
- Optional Apple-authenticated global and per-puzzle leaderboards, verified by a Cloudflare Workers + D1 service.
- My combines local Progress and Account, with public nickname settings, explicit solution sharing and account deletion.

PURPOSE AND ACCESS
An educational programming puzzle game for learners and developers. Guide a robot to light every target on a 25 x 25 board using H: s (forward), l (left) and r (right). Procedures, parameters and recursion allow very short solutions. Each letter and numeric literal costs one byte; punctuation is free. All 50 puzzles, hints, the Guide and progress work offline. Offline play needs no account. Optional leaderboards offer Apple sign-in, nickname settings and account deletion when a service URL is configured. No ads, tracking, purchases or paid app features.

HOW TO TEST
1. Problems: open L01, type s (or tap the s key), then Run. On iPhone, scroll the editor header near the top before typing. L02 can be solved with rsslsslss. "Show the next hint" reveals one of two hints; "Game rules" explains H.
2. Search L31 or L41 to find the new chapters; L50 is the final puzzle. All puzzles are available without payment or unlocking earlier chapters.
3. In any puzzle, open Board settings and choose 3D. Close settings, then use Enlarge board. Try rotate, tilt, zoom and follow-robot controls; execution controls remain available. Close to return to the editor. Modern and Classic are still available.
4. Guide explains H and shows version/build at the end. My > Progress lists solved puzzles and exports/imports JSON backups.
5. AI Battlefield: open AI Providers ("Models" on iPhone), add a provider with an HTTPS base URL and your own API key, then save to load models. Under New match ("New" on iPhone), choose models and puzzles and tap "Agree & start match". While generating, the stop button at the end of an answer's first line cancels that answer; the match continues. The same action offers retry afterwards. Open an answer, view history or share a result image.

EXTERNAL SERVICES AND PRIVACY
Optional AI Battlefield uses providers configured by the user: OpenRouter, SiliconFlow, Google Gemini, or an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible HTTPS API. Saving a provider fetches models; consenting to a match sends rules, selected boards, an optional prompt and AI conversation directly to that provider. A provider account/key is required for AI requests and the provider may charge for usage. Keys are stored in the system Keychain, never bundled. AI requests go directly to the selected provider, without a developer proxy. The optional leaderboard uses the configured Worker service and Apple authentication; no analytics or payment service is integrated. Answers are judged locally; H programs move the robot and never execute as native code. Product-page match screenshots use demo models.

REGIONS AND CONTENT
Features are identical across available regions; UI languages are English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. All bundled puzzles, texts and artwork are original, MIT-licensed at https://github.com/hugogu/herbert. The app independently implements the public Herbert rules and is not affiliated with Wild Noodle or Microsoft. No community puzzle archive or reference solutions are bundled.

OPTIONAL LEADERBOARD
When a service URL is configured, My > Account provides Sign in with Apple, nickname editing and account deletion. Puzzle pages offer explicit solution uploads; the server replays programs to verify scores. Only nicknames and verified scores/metrics are public, not solution programs. Offline play still needs no account.

Public nicknames are screened. Ranking menus offer Report nickname (email draft) and Hide player, with undo. Operators can reset names, hide scores or block users with audit records.
