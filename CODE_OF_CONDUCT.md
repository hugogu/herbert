# Code of conduct

Herbert welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Treat people with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and code.
- Be patient with questions and differences in language, experience, or accessibility needs.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish someone else's private information.
- Avoid personal attacks, sexualized conduct, spam, and persistent unwanted contact.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation
when conduct disrupts a welcoming environment. Enforcement should consider the context
and severity, explain the decision where appropriate, and protect reporters' privacy.

Report concerns privately to hugogu@outlook.com. Include links or relevant context without
posting private information publicly. Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer; anyone
involved in a report should not decide its outcome alone.
